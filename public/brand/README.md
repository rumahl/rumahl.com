# rumahl brandmark assets

These files are the supplied rumahl brandmark, exported as SVG. Do not redraw,
trace, stretch or re-typeset the mark. The published ZIP at
<https://rumahl.com/brand/rumahl-brandmark.zip> contains the same files.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `rumahl-mark.svg` | The “r” monogram in rumahl green |
| `rumahl-wordmark.svg` | The full wordmark in rumahl green |
| `rumahl-mark-black.svg` / `rumahl-wordmark-black.svg` | One-colour ink reproduction |
| `rumahl-mark-white.svg` / `rumahl-wordmark-white.svg` | One-colour light reproduction on dark surfaces |
| `rumahl-app-icon.svg` | Square app-icon treatment |
| `rumahl-avatar-light.svg` / `rumahl-avatar-dark.svg` / `rumahl-avatar-sand.svg` | Square avatars safe for a round crop |
| `rumahl-colors.css` / `rumahl-colors.json` | Approved tint formulas, default colours and palette values |

The name and brandmark are governed by the trademark policy at
<https://rumahl.com/legal/trademark>. The source code is Apache-2.0; the
brandmark is not automatically licensed for every use.

The monogram and the wordmark are separate marks. Never combine them into a
lockup. The wordmark uses the r shape nine times: once as the r, twice in the u
(rotated and joined), four times in the m, once on the right of the a and once
as the right part of the h. The final l is the straight stem. Any single solid
colour is allowed as long as the whole mark keeps that one colour value; on
photography use white or black only. Keep the clear space and minimum sizes
documented at <https://rumahl.com/brand>.

Partner and service marks are not part of this asset set. Use the respective
project's original files and colours, show them separately from the rumahl
mark, and never merge them into a lockup.

Product logos such as `r | Design` and `r | OS` are not published as assets.
They are reserved for official rumahl product surfaces and may not be used by
third parties.
