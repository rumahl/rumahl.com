# Partner logo assets

## Home Assistant

- File: `home-assistant-logomark-color.svg`
- Source: <https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/brand-assets/tree/main/home-assistant/logo/screen/logomark>
- Guidelines: <https://contributors.home-assistant.io/brand/name-and-logo/>
- Retrieved: 2026-11-10
- Use: the official full-colour logomark on a light background, for identification only. Home Assistant permits the logomark without the wordmark where the context makes the brand clear.

Home Assistant is a trademark of the Open Home Foundation. Its use does not
imply endorsement or partnership. Commercial use requires written permission
from the Open Home Foundation: partner@openhomefoundation.org.

Follow the Home Assistant guidelines: write the name in full with capital H and
A, use the official lockup, do not put the logomark in a square or coloured
container, do not recolour or outline it, and keep at least a quarter of the
logomark height as clear space.
