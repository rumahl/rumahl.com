# Photography and image policy

rumahl supports human photographers and artists. Do not generate images with
AI or use generatively created or generatively altered stock imagery.

Choose photographs for their connection to the adjacent content: local
hardware, home networking, storage, development and people working on their
own systems. Do not use unrelated scenery, architecture or decorative stock
photography simply to fill a layout.

For now, use traceable photographs from Unsplash. Prefer original photo pages
with a named photographer, publication history and camera information. Inspect
the actual downloaded image. These records support provenance; visual
appearance alone does not prove how an image was made.

Record the photo page, photographer, license, download URL, verification date
and file checksum in `content/photo-sources.json`. Keep visible attribution
linked to the original photo. Download fixed, optimized images locally; do
not use random image endpoints or hotlink images on every page visit.

Do not present stock photos as rumahl screenshots, certified hardware,
members of the project, or particular product models. For a hardware listing,
use a verified photo of that exact model or the existing neutral device icon.
Unverified legacy product photos are not used.

Future original photography should replace stock photos in the same central
registry. Keep the photographer credit, permission record and relevant
context. Real screenshots may be captured from the running software; never
fabricate product screenshots with generative tools.
